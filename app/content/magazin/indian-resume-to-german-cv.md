An Indian resume and a German CV share most of their content, so you don't have to start again. You remove a few things German readers find odd (the declaration, the biodata, CTC), rewrite the career objective as a short profile, and add three things they look for: language levels, visa status and an explanation of your grades. This article walks through a complete example. The person is fictional, the conventions are real.

Indian career sites describe these elements as typical. Naukri calls the closing declaration "commonly seen in Indian-style resumes", and biodata with parents' names, gender and marital status are standard for government jobs and traditional companies. That is not a mistake in India. It is simply different from what a recruiter in Munich or Berlin expects.

## The starting point

Here is the top of a resume as it might arrive, from a backend developer with five years of experience:

```text
RESUME

ARJUN MEHTA
Mobile: +91 98xxx xxxxx | Email: arjun.mehta@example.com

CAREER OBJECTIVE
To work in a challenging environment where I can utilise my skills
and contribute to the growth of the organisation.

PERSONAL DETAILS
Father's Name: Rajesh Mehta
Date of Birth: 12/03/1995   Gender: Male   Marital Status: Single
Religion: Hindu   Nationality: Indian
Passport No.: Z1234567
Languages Known: English, Hindi, Gujarati

ACADEMIC QUALIFICATIONS
B.Tech (Computer Science), 2017, CGPA 8.2
Class XII (CBSE), 2013, 89%
Class X (CBSE), 2011, 9.4 CGPA

EXPERIENCE SUMMARY
Senior Software Engineer, Example Tech Pvt Ltd, Pune (Jul'21 – Till Date)
- Responsible for development of microservices
...
Current CTC: 18 LPA

STRENGTHS
Hardworking, quick learner, team player

DECLARATION
I hereby declare that the above information is true to the best of my knowledge.
Place: Pune
```

## Step 1: Remove what German readers don't expect

- **"RESUME" as a title.** Not needed. If you want a heading, "Curriculum Vitae" or "CV" is fine, but your name is the real title.
- **Father's name, gender, religion, marital status.** The Federal Employment Agency's guide for international applicants says marital status and religion "are not required". Parents' names have no place in a German CV.
- **Passport number.** Never on a CV. It is sensitive data, and you will only be asked for it during the visa process.
- **Class X and Class XII.** Once you have a degree, school results are rarely relevant. Keep at most one line for your school-leaving certificate.
- **CTC.** German employers talk about annual gross salary in euros, and only when they ask. Salary expectations go into the cover letter, if the ad asks for them.
- **Strengths.** Words like "hardworking" carry no information on their own. The ZAV recommends putting soft skills in the cover letter, with an example.
- **Declaration.** In Germany it goes without saying that your CV is truthful. A declaration looks out of place.

## Step 2: Rewrite the objective as a profile

The career objective tells the reader what you want. A German profile tells them what you offer, in two or three sentences:

```text
Backend engineer with five years of experience building Java and Kotlin
microservices on AWS for a payments platform with two million users.
Looking for a backend role in Germany; EU Blue Card eligible, German B1.
```

Be specific: technologies, scale, domain, and one sentence on what you are looking for.

## Step 3: Add what German readers look for

**Language levels on the CEFR scale.** "Languages Known: English, Hindi, Gujarati" doesn't say how well. Make it in Germany recommends the European levels:

```text
English   C1
German    B1 (Goethe-Zertifikat, 2026)
Hindi     native
Gujarati  native
```

**Work authorisation and availability.** One line each saves the employer a question:

```text
Work authorisation   Eligible for EU Blue Card (degree comparable, anabin H+)
Availability         Notice period 3 months
Location             Pune, relocating to Germany
```

Only write "anabin H+" if you have checked it. Your university's status alone isn't enough; the degree has to be listed as equivalent too. If it isn't, the ZAB issues a Statement of Comparability.

**Grades with an explanation.** "CGPA 8.2" means little to a German reader. Write the original and the German equivalent using the modified Bavarian formula. Because the minimum pass grade differs between Indian universities, there is no single conversion table. The method is in [how to show your CGPA on a German CV](/en/magazine/cgpa-german-cv/).

## Step 4: Rewrite the experience

German CVs use month and year and put the newest job first. "Jul'21 – Till Date" becomes "07/2021 – present". Bullets should describe results rather than duties:

```text
07/2021 – present   Senior Software Engineer
                    Example Tech Pvt Ltd, Pune (fintech, 400 employees)
                    • Split a monolithic billing service into six Kotlin
                      microservices; deployment time from 2 hours to 15 minutes
                    • Introduced contract tests between eight services
                    • Mentored three junior developers
```

"Responsible for development of microservices" tells the reader your job title. The rewritten version tells them what you achieved.

The personal details are gone, the content has stayed. With the bullets in full, this fits on one to two pages.

## When it isn't your CV

Changing the format helps, but it is not the only reason applications from abroad go unanswered. Jobs that explicitly need no German are rare: Indeed's Hiring Lab found that only 2.7 percent of job ads posted on Indeed in Germany were marked as not requiring German (September 2023 to August 2024), about 3.7 percent in software development. That is platform data from one optional field, so treat it as an indication. If you get no replies despite a good CV, a German course can matter more than a new layout.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I remove the declaration from my CV for Germany?

Yes. German employers assume your CV is truthful, and a declaration at the end looks unusual. Use the space for a line on your work authorisation or availability instead.

### Do German employers want my father's name or marital status?

No. The Federal Employment Agency says marital status and religion are not required, and parents' names are not part of a German CV.

### Should I put my salary on my CV?

No. If the job ad asks for salary expectations, give an annual gross amount in euros in the cover letter. CTC in lakhs means little to a German employer.

### Do I need to include Class X and XII marks?

Not once you have a degree. A single line for your school-leaving certificate is enough, or none at all if your CV is already long.
