This file only exists for the check scripts. It has no German counterpart, so it must not get links to other language versions.

German employers usually expect a CV, a cover letter and copies of certificates. Translations of certificates are often requested; ask before you pay for certified ones.

## Recognition of qualifications

For regulated professions, recognition is required before you can work. For other jobs it helps employers judge a foreign degree.
